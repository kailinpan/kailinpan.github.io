Announcements:

Normed equivariant ring spectra and the $\mathbb{A}_n$-modules. Joint work in progress with Zhenpeng Li.

Abstract. In this paper, we analyze the difference between normed equivariant ring spectra and commutative equivariant ring spectra, and show that the extra data in between are essentially finite.
  More precisely, given a finite group $G$, let $T$ be the monad for the normed algebras in $\EuScript{S}p_G$ over the commutative algebras. Then we prove that the category of normed algebras in $\EuScript{S}p_G$ is equivalent to the category of $\mathbb{A}_n$-modules over $T$, where $n$ is the maximum length of chains of subgroups in $G$.
  Along the way, we establish an $\infty$-categorical obstruction theory of $\mathbb{A}_n$-algebras and $\mathbb{A}_n$-modules, which may be of independent interest. In conclusion, a normed algebra can be built from its underlying commutative algebra along with finitely many additional structure maps and higher homotopy coherences.
  In particular, when $G=C_p$ is a cyclic group of prime order, we provide an alternative proof of Yang's result on normed $C_p$-equivariant ring spectra.

Constant spectral Mackey functors and the equivariant Steenrod algebra. [arXiv]

Abstract. For a finite group $G$, we define a constant spectral $G$-Mackey functor as a genuine $G$-spectrum in which the restrictions between categorical fixed points are equivalences. We further identify the full subcategory of constant spectral $G$-Mackey functors with the category of modules over a certain $\mathbb{E}_1$-ring spectrum $R_G$.
  When $G=C_{p^n}$ is a cyclic $p$-group, we provide an explicit construction of the $\mathbb{E}_1$-ring spectrum $R_{C_{p^n}}$. The proof uses a reconstruction theorem for genuine $C_{p^n}$-spectra, which might be of independent interest. As an application, we compute the $\mathbb{Z}$-graded $C_{p^n}$-equivariant mod $p$ Steenrod algebra.
